New topic at the right level Passes

Parking permits

Permits are sold by semester or for the full year.

Permit prices

Prices depend on the lot, from $150 to $600 a year.

Contact Commuter Services

Call, email, or visit the Commuter Services office.

"Permit prices" is part of "Parking permits," so it sits one level below. "Contact Commuter Services" is its own topic, so it moves back up to the same level as "Parking permits."

A bulleted list Passes

To buy a permit, you'll need:

  • Your uNID
  • Your vehicle's license plate number
  • A credit card or campus account

Made with the editor's list button. Screen readers announce "list, 3 items." Bullets, because the order doesn't matter.

A numbered list Passes

How to buy a permit:

  1. Log in to the Commuter Services portal.
  2. Choose a permit type and lot.
  3. Pay online. Your permit is linked to your license plate.

Numbers, because the steps happen in order. The editor adds the numbers and keeps them correct when a step is added or removed.

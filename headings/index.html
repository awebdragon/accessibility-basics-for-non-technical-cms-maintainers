Bold text used as a heading Fails

Parking permits

Permits are sold by semester or for the full year. Prices depend on the lot.

It looks like a heading, but it's a bold paragraph. It won't appear in a screen reader's list of headings, so people can't jump to it.

Heading used to make text bigger Fails

Permit sales open August 1. Buy early to get your first-choice lot!

Permits are sold by semester or for the full year. Prices depend on the lot.

This is an announcement, not the title of a section. Screen reader users jumping between headings land on it and expect a new topic.

New topic placed under the wrong heading Fails

Parking permits

Permits are sold by semester or for the full year.

Permit prices

Prices depend on the lot, from $150 to $600 a year.

Contact Commuter Services

Call, email, or visit the Commuter Services office.

"Contact Commuter Services" isn't part of "Parking permits," but its level puts it there. Test: list only the headings, indented like a table of contents. Does each one sit under the right parent?

Headings that don't describe their section Fails

Overview

Free shuttles run between the Union, the hospital, and Research Park every 10 minutes.

Details

Shuttles run 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekdays when classes are in session.

More

Riders who need a lift-equipped shuttle can request one by phone.

In a list of headings, "Overview, Details, More" tells someone nothing about what's on the page.

Cards that skip a heading level Passes

Study rooms

Reserve a quiet room at the Marriott Library for up to four hours.

Reserve a study room

Campus map

Find buildings, parking, and accessible entrances across campus.

Open the campus map

IT help desk

Get help with passwords, Wi-Fi, and campus software.

Contact the IT help desk

Most scanners flag this because the cards jump from a level 2 heading to level 4. It still passes: each card is a subtopic of the heading above it, so the structure is right.

Bullets typed with dashes Fails

To buy a permit, you'll need:

- Your uNID
- Your vehicle's license plate number
- A credit card or campus account

It looks like a list, but it's one paragraph. Screen readers don't say "list, 3 items," so listeners don't know how many things are coming.

Steps numbered by hand Fails

How to buy a permit:

1. Log in to the Commuter Services portal.

2. Choose a permit type and lot.

3. Pay online. Your permit is linked to your license plate.

Three separate paragraphs that happen to start with numbers. Nothing tells a screen reader they're steps, or how many there are.

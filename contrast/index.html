WCAG success criteria
  • 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)
  • 1.4.1 Use of Color (Level A)
Who is most affected
  • People with low vision
  • People with color vision deficiency (color blindness)
  • Older adults, as contrast sensitivity fades with age
  • Anyone reading in glare, bright sun, or on a dim screen

White text on a light brand color Fails

Finals week hours: the Marriott Library is open 24 hours a day, Sunday through Thursday.

Text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast with its background. White on this gold is 1.73:1.

White text on a button Fails

Book a study room

Button text follows the same 4.5:1 rule, measured against the button's own color. White on this green is 2.22:1.

Links shown by color alone Fails

Need a quiet place to study? You can reserve a room on the Marriott Library website, find the building on the campus map, or contact the IT help desk if you have trouble logging in.

With no underline, the only clue that these are links is a dark red that barely differs from the black text (2.06:1).

Low-contrast text in an image Fails

Text inside an image has to meet the same 4.5:1 rule (logos are the exception). Testing tools can't read the image, so check it with a color picker.

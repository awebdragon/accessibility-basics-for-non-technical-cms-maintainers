WCAG success criteria
  • 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)
  • 1.4.1 Use of Color (Level A)
Who is most affected
  • People with low vision
  • People with color vision deficiency (color blindness)
  • Older adults, as contrast sensitivity fades with age
  • Anyone reading in glare, bright sun, or on a dim screen

Black text on the same brand color Passes

Finals week hours: the Marriott Library is open 24 hours a day, Sunday through Thursday.

Same gold background, dark text instead of white: 12.13:1. Light brand colors usually need dark text.

Black text on the same button color Passes

Book a study room

Same green button, dark text: 9.45:1. The dark border means the green isn't the only thing showing where the button is.

Fix A: links are underlined Passes

Need a quiet place to study? You can reserve a room on the Marriott Library website, find the building on the campus map, or contact the IT help desk if you have trouble logging in.

The underline marks the links, so their color no longer matters (it still needs 4.5:1 against the background, like any text).
